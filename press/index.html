// Full Description

About the game

Electric Crust: Galactic Pizza Delivery is a tight, replayable arcade shooter built around fast side-scrolling combat and a deliberately strange premise: the galaxy's best pilot, delivering pizza. Ace flies for Chef Rex across a 15-stage campaign, dodging asteroid fields and enemy fire while boss battles escalate in scale and difficulty across three tiers.

A warp mechanic lets players push through dangerous stretches at high risk and high reward, and a climbing Federation rank system offers Ace a way out of delivery work entirely — a promotion, a command, a way up. She turns it down every time. The job matters more than the rank.

Electric Crust is built solo in Unity by Swamp City LLC, an independent studio in Northwest Arkansas.
