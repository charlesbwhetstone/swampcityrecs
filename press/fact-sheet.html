Galactic Pizza Delivery — Swamp City LLC

// Boilerplate

Short description

Electric Crust: Galactic Pizza Delivery is a retro side-scrolling arcade space shooter from Swamp City LLC. Players pilot Ace through a 15-stage campaign across asteroid fields, neon nebulas, and enemy fleets, delivering pizza for Chef Rex while battling tiered bosses, activating warp mechanics, and climbing a Federation rank ladder she'd rather not accept. Electric Crust launches on iOS and Android January 26, 2027, with Steam to follow.
