// Writing

The story

Cherokee Outlet, Indian Territory. 1879.

Three cattle are torn apart in a single night, and the tracks don’t match anything Marshal Gideon Payne has seen in twenty years of work. A farmhand goes missing. Claw marks appear on the wall of the hardware store, six feet off the ground.

Hilde Brauer, a German widow farming alone since her husband died, recognizes the print at her fence line. Her grandmother drew that exact shape in a journal sixty-seven pages long, brought across the ocean and never meant to be needed here.

And a stranger named Deward Osburn rides into Wolf Creek carrying a silver-handled knife, two silver bullets, and a question he has refused to answer for sixteen years.

What none of them know yet is that the man responsible is the most decent soul in the territory, a rancher who gives generously, remembers every name, and wakes some mornings covered in blood he cannot account for.

The curse did not come from this land. It was carried here, like everything else.

Blood Moon at Wolf Creek is a horror-western in three voices, set on ground the U.S. Army took by force within the decade, where the newest monster is the one that came west in a crate.
