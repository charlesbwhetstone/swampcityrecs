// About

About Swamp City

Swamp City is my independent studio in Northwest Arkansas, a home for radical weirdness. Forensics Engineer by day, DIY enthusiast by night. Retro at heart, built around feel, a little off-kilter by design.

Arcade games are where I'm starting: compact, replayable, tight. Music and other odd projects are fair game too, just me, chasing whatever's worth making.

// Now in development

Electric Crust: Galactic Pizza Delivery

My debut title is a retro side-scrolling space shooter. Pilot Ace blasts through a 15-stage campaign across asteroid fields, neon nebulas, and enemy fleets, delivering pizza for Chef Rex across the galaxy. Tiered boss battles, warp mechanics, and a climbing rank system keep every run fresh.
