About Swamp City

Swamp City is a small independent studio in Northwest Arkansas — a home for radical weirdness. Retro at heart, built around feel, intentionally strange-leaning.

Arcade games are where we're starting: compact, replayable, tight. Music and other odd projects are fair game too. A small team chasing whatever's worth making.

// Now in development

Electric Crust: Galactic Pizza Delivery

Our debut title is a retro side-scrolling space shooter. Pilot Ace blasts through a 15-stage campaign — asteroid fields, neon nebulas, enemy fleets — delivering pizza for Chef Rex across the galaxy. Tiered boss battles, warp mechanics, and a climbing rank system keep every run fresh.
